SHA TRACKER — HOW TO START

This kit helps you get ready for your Separation Health Assessment (SHA) and
VA disability claim. Claude reads your medical records (after removing your
personal information), asks you the SHA questions one at a time, and builds
an Excel tracker of your likely conditions and estimated VA rating.

What you need: a computer, your MHS GENESIS Patient Portal login, and a paid
Claude account.

1. Install the Claude desktop app: https://claude.ai/download — sign in.
2. Unzip SHA-Toolkit.zip somewhere easy to find, like your Desktop.
   (Right-click the zip → Extract All.)
3. Open Claude, click "Code", and choose the SHA-Toolkit folder you just unzipped.
4. Type:  start

Claude takes it from there. It will ask before installing anything — click
Approve when it does. You can stop any time; type "resume" later to pick up
where you left off.

Your privacy: Claude never opens your original records. Your name, SSN, DoD ID
and birth date go into a file on your own computer that only the cleanup scripts
read — never into the chat.

This is a preparation tool, not legal or medical advice. Take the finished
tracker to a free accredited VSO (DAV, VFW, American Legion, AMVETS, PVA).
